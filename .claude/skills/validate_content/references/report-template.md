Write to `status/content/validate_content_<timestamp>.md` (path from
`--report-path`). Keep the section order — these reports are read side by side with older
ones to see what moved.

Drop a section only when it is empty, and say so rather than deleting it silently.

Three to six sentences. What is in good shape, what has drifted the furthest, and the one

## 🔴 Critical

One block per finding — **not** a table. A real fix is often two sentences of replacement prose or
a corrected line of code, which a table cell cannot hold legibly.

**<ID> · 🔴 <short label>** — `colab/class_4_ViT_and_FD.ipynb` cells 64, 65, 72

- **Source:** <url> · verified

IDs are per-notebook and stable within a report — `C1-01`, `V4-03`, `B7-12`. They let the per-notebook

Group the section by theme when it runs long (e.g. "Runtime blockers" / "Wrong explanations") —

## Conventions

- **Cite cells, always.** `cell 42` is what makes a finding actionable; a finding without one
  is a vague opinion.
- **One block per finding**, even when the same problem appears in several notebooks. Put a
  finding that spans notebooks in a **Cross-cutting findings** section right after the summary,
  with every location listed — those are the highest-leverage fixes and burying them inside one
  notebook's section hides that. Say in the Method section how many duplicates were collapsed.
- **Quote the notebook** in "what it says now", trimmed to the clause that is wrong. Do not
  paraphrase it into something more defensible than what is written.
- **Suggested fix is concrete**: the replacement sentence, the new checkpoint id, the exact
  section to insert. "Update this" is not a fix.
- **Match the notebook's language** in quotes and suggested replacement text — Spanish class,
  Spanish fix. The report's own prose can be English.
